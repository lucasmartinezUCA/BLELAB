## Procedural (built in code, not from files)
Executive desk, office chairs, guest armchairs, bookcases and ~1,500 instanced books, reading table, book stand, meeting tables, reception counter, credenzas, filing cabinets, monitors, keyboard, phone, mug, pen cup, legal pad, folders, banker’s lamps, windows, doors, casings, ceiling lights, whiteboards, wall displays, pinboard, court models, artwork, signage. Status: **temporary** for the photorealism target, except the court models (architectural maquettes are white-board models in reality).

## Missing for full ArchViz photorealism (priority order)
1. Executive desk and ergonomic office chair, GLB with PBR textures (e.g. Poly Haven “Furniture”, CC0; or commissioned).
2. Bookcase GLB and photographed book spines.
3. Monitor, keyboard, desk phone GLB.
4. Photographic PBR materials: walnut veneer, oak planks, leather, wool fabric, painted plaster (ambientCG or Poly Haven, CC0, 2K).
5. Interior HDRI (Poly Haven, CC0) for reflections.
6. Client portraits (licensed photographs) or realistic avatars; the dialog currently shows a provisional monogram portrait.
The loader is ready for these: add the file to assets/models and an entry in `dyn.glb` (js/world.js).

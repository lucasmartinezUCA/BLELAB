## Notes on the client models
- **client_dana.glb depicts a well-known film character (John Wick) and resembles a real actor.** The uploader's CC BY licence covers the 3D file, not the film studio's character rights or the actor's likeness. For a published commercial course, replace it with a generic rigged model before release. It is a drop-in swap: same rig, same file name.
- The walk/idle/gesture clips are retargeted at load time (world-space, with rest-pose correction), and the seated pose is procedural, so any Mixamo-style rig works.
